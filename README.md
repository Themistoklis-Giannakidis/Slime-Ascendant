# Slime-Ascendant
🫧 Slime Ascendant
A 3D low-poly survival game where you start as a tiny slime in a vast world — gather resources, craft tools, build shelter, survive waves of enemies, and evolve into something greater.

🌿 Concept
You begin as a small, vulnerable slime with nothing. The world is bigger than you. Through gathering, crafting, and surviving, you grow stronger — and eventually evolve beyond your original form.
The vision is a full survival loop where evolution is the reward for mastering each stage of the game.

🎮 Gameplay (Planned)

🪨 Gather — collect resources from the environment
🔨 Craft — build tools and equipment to survive
🏗️ Build — construct shelter and structures
🌊 Survive — fight off waves of enemies
🧬 Evolve — grow and transform as you progress
